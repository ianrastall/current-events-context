# Expanded daily files (synthesis layer)

This tree holds schema-2.2 expansions of selected days: the Wikipedia Current
Events portal bullets for a date, enriched with a deep-research report. These
are synthesis artifacts. They are kept apart from the seed files in
`<YYYY>/<MM>/<YYYY-MM-DD>.yaml`, which are extraction output and can be
regenerated without touching anything here.

Layout: `expanded/<YYYY>/<MM>/<YYYY-MM-DD>.yaml`. Shape and rules:
`reference/schema/daily-events.schema.json` and
`reference/schema/AUTHORING_GUIDE.md`. Validate with:

```
python reference/schema/validate.py expanded/2026/01/2026-01-06.yaml
```

## Current contents

| Dates | Status | Origin |
|---|---|---|
| 2026-01-01 to 2026-01-05 | reviewed | Restored unchanged from commit `0f5cbef6` (identical to the published copies) |
| 2026-01-06 to 2026-01-12, 2026-03-05 to 2026-03-09, 2026-03-12 to 2026-03-18 | draft | Built on 2026-09-22 with `reference/expansion/build.py` |

The 19 draft files were generated by Claude Opus 5.5 (`claude-opus-5-5`) from:

- the portal page at the revision recorded in the day's seed file, cached in
  `reference/expansion/wikitext/` with its revision ID, timestamp and SHA-256;
- the deep-research report in `reference/deep-research/<YYYY>/<MM>/`;
- for some March days, the earlier schema-2.1 expansion from commit `0f5cbef6`;
- the judgment overlays in `reference/expansion/overlays/` (portal matching,
  importance, headlines for portal-only events, corrections and exclusions).

Each file's `dataset.scope.seed_source` names its exact inputs. Every portal
bullet of the pinned revision appears in the file exactly once, either as the
`text_fragment` of an enriched event or as a portal-only event.

2026-03-10 and 2026-03-11 have schema-2.1 expansions in commit `0f5cbef6` but no
deep-research report, so they are not included here.

## Known limitations of the draft files

- Deep-research reports are model output. Items were kept as reported unless
  there was concrete evidence of misdating; they have not been fact-checked
  against their sources.
- Excluded as misdated or recycled (listed in `drop_md` in the overlays):
  2026-01-07, Iranian missile strikes on Israel and the UAE (sole source dated
  2026-03-17, before the war began); 2026-03-06, four Thailand and Mandiant items
  whose cited articles date from about 2012 to 2021.
- 2026-03-15 does not use its deep-research report. The report describes a
  major San Francisco Bay Area earthquake with 60+ deaths whose citations are
  articles about earthquakes in Mexico, and its other items do not match the
  portal. The day is built from its schema-2.1 expansion instead. 2026-03-06 and
  2026-03-13 likewise use their 2.1 expansions, because their reports are
  narrative or do not describe that day's events.
- Flagged but kept with low confidence: 2026-01-07 Vietnam–Russia item (source
  dated 2026-03-25); 2026-03-06 EU–Mercosur animal-welfare item (blog
  commentary).
- The 2026-01-10 report gives outlet names but no URLs. Those events have no
  external sources; the named outlets are listed in `notes`.
- When a source gave no publication date, the date in its URL is used, or
  failing that the file date.
- `enriched_manually: true` follows the existing convention for events enriched
  beyond the portal bullet; the enrichment here is model-generated.
