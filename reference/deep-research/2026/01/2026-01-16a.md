## Event: Canada and China reach Beijing trade agreement cutting tariffs on Chinese electric vehicles and Canadian canola
* **Category & Subcategory:** Business and economy / International trade agreements
* **Geography:** China, Canada; Beijing
* **Summary:** Canadian Prime Minister Mark Carney and Chinese President Xi Jinping concluded a preliminary trade and strategic partnership agreement during a summit in Beijing on January 16, marking the first visit to China by a Canadian prime minister since 2017. Under the accord, Canada agreed to replace its 100% surtax on Chinese-built electric vehicles with an annual tariff-rate quota allowing up to 49,000 Chinese EVs into the Canadian market at the most-favored-nation tariff rate of 6.1%. In exchange, China committed to lower its combined tariffs on Canadian canola seed from roughly 84% to approximately 15% by March 1, 2026, and to suspend anti-discrimination duties on Canadian canola meal, lobsters, crabs, and peas from March 1 through at least the end of 2026.
